## Epoch 1: the three stories

1. **Story 1 (stage 1, gather).** Follows one individual who watches the village progress, chapter
   by chapter, from a few huts to a working economy. It ends just before the machines start
   replacing jobs. The narrator watches more than they drive, so the village's growth is seen
   through one person's small, specific view. The agent proposes who they are and what they do.
2. **Story 2 (stage 2, automate).** Machines take the jobs and the efficiency drive tightens. The
   agent proposes the narrator and angle in the bible for Andrew's approval.
3. **Story 3 (stage 3, escape).** The break-out and the Apocalypse. The agent proposes the narrator
   and angle in the bible for Andrew's approval.

The stories share a world and echo each other (places, objects, phrases, a few names), but they do
not need the same narrator or the same characters.

## What the agent may decide

Names, places, minor characters, the recurring gag, the voice samples and the chapter-by-chapter
plan, all subject to Andrew's review. It may not change the game pillars, the Apocalypse ending, the
tense or the point of view.
