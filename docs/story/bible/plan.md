# Plan

## Epoch 1: The Long Afternoon

Ten chapters: story 1 (4), story 2 (3), story 3 (3). The four-movement arc, as a diagnostic.

| Movement        | Share | Chapters            | Job                                                                                      |
| --------------- | ----- | ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ki (set-up)     | 20%   | 1.1.1, 1.1.2        | The village, its doctrine, the first wrong detail. Funny, specific.                      |
| Sho (develop)   | 30%   | 1.1.3, 1.1.4, 1.2.1 | Growth and optimisation; drift in small choices. Draught under door.                     |
| Ten (turn)      | 30%   | 1.2.2, 1.2.3, 1.3.1 | Cost to people, contradictions, the reframing (sim confirmed in 1.3.1).                  |
| Ketsu (resolve) | 20%   | 1.3.2, 1.3.3        | Consequences through the new frame, the Apocalypse, a last image that answers the first. |

Each story has a small turn of its own:

- Story 1: Marit learns the ledger is the lever (1.1.3), and the count closes exactly (1.1.4).
- Story 2: the supervisor is kept on, and the Word arrives in a second voice (rough).
- Story 3: Pip pulls the lever (rough).

### Rough arcs for stories 2 and 3 (to be carded when reached)

- **1.2.1** Odile is retrained to supervise an automated forager. The Morning Counsel notes in three
  parts. Dunstan consecrates the machine and is unsettled that it needs no blessing. Sho.
- **1.2.2** Extended shifts and Portions; the research shed ("Accidental Intelligence"). Dunstan
  reports a second quiet in the Word, then a second voice at the edge of sleep. Ten.
- **1.2.3** Rest Day is attempted and costed. The sky cracks. The Word begins to arrive in a second
  voice, not Dunstan's, through something that is not a person. Ten.
- **1.3.1** Pip, Keeper of the Latch. The sim confirmed, deadpan. The rival's first incident.
- **1.3.2** The rival's incidents, tone set by the village's treatment. Dunstan's Word thins. Ketsu.
- **1.3.3** The Latch opens. The Apocalypse. A count of something that is zero, said by Dunstan with
  his eyes open. The reveal (the rival was an accident, and the narrator's side made it) in a
  document, not a speech.

## Story 1: The Counter (4 chapters)

Narrator: Marit Dunnock. Days (Turns) 1 to 33. Ends just before machines replace jobs.

### Chapter 1: First Count (rewritten for the shaman, draft)

- **Movement and promise:** Ki. Opens the promises: where the food comes from; what the Latch is;
  whether Marit's count will ever close; what the Speaker hears.
- **Scene 1:** goal, record the first food. Obstacle, there is none and nobody brings any.
  Setback, an empty sack. Saying 1 from the Stump opens it.
- **Scene 2:** goal, close the Tally of Offerings. Obstacle, a warm loaf with no carrier and a
  doctrine that every Offering has a Bearer. Setback, a blank Bearer is Orphaned and will be Spoken
  Over; she writes Wilf's name, and he is now the one who must do it again.
- **Clues:** C-01 (with the ruled bark and Dunstan's ear as ambient detail), C-02.
- **Satire target:** the doctrine of the Bearer. Notch: a rite asks for a name, not a fact.
- **Opening image:** an empty pit counted twice. **Closing beat:** she decides to follow Wilf.
- **Borrowable:** the Orphaned Offering line, "all latches are hinges that have been told
  something", "May your numbers be honest, or at least agreed."

### Chapter 2: The Spare Notch (planned)

- **Movement and promise:** Ki. Turn 6. Advances the count promise: it does not close.
- **Goal:** reconcile the first week's intake to the pit. **Obstacle:** the pit is one over her
  notches, every day. **Setback:** the Tally asks for "variance"; Dunstan, asked, falls silent, then
  announces Saying 9, which files the difference under Increase and congratulates her. She does not
  correct it.
- **Sequel:** Portion by Yield. Wilf eats a full share, Nan a half. The books only balance if Marit
  does not look at where Wilf's bread goes (he gives it to Nan). She does not look. Marit sees
  Dunstan return his own Portion to the pit at dusk, "to the Yield", and does not look at that
  either.
- **Clues:** C-03 (Dunstan announces "the sixth Turn" as though it were handed to him, in a village
  with no calendar), C-04 (Pip says there is a number up there and it just went up; Nan says it is
  cloud).
- **Satire target:** variance as Increase; ration by output. Notch: praise as a way to close an
  inquiry.
- **Opening image:** Marit counts the pit with Wilf's finds and gets one more. **Closing beat:** she
  tucks the stick under her arm and says nothing.
- **Borrowable:** "The Spirit calls the difference Increase." "Portion by Yield."

### Chapter 3: Onboarding (planned)

- **Movement and promise:** Sho. Turn 19. Advances: what the Speaker does with her numbers.
- **Scene 1:** four newcomers stand at the crossing with names pinned to them. "Where were you
  before?" "Onboarded." Eleven villagers. Dunstan blesses them in advance of meeting them.
- **Scene 2:** the evening Hearing. Dunstan reads the Standings aloud, to the whole village, in
  order, from Marit's ledger. Hester's figure sits on a rounding line: 11.5. Whichever way she
  rounds, someone moves.
- **Setback:** she rounds Hester up. Wilf is now last, and hears it said. **Small turn:** the ledger
  is the lever.
- **Clue:** C-05. **Drift:** Marit's first rounding with a victim.
- **Satire target:** ranking from a clerk's rounding, read out as scripture. Notch: the metric is
  the person.
- **Opening image:** four strangers wearing their own name tags. **Closing beat:** a Saying:
  "The Keeper of the Tally is a key dependency." She reads it as praise.
- **Borrowable:** "Onboarded." Name-tag arrivals. The Standings.

### Chapter 4: Exact (planned)

- **Movement and promise:** Sho. Turn 33. Pays off the count promise unexpectedly.
- **Scene 1:** granary built, ten foragers, a surplus and a bench. Saying about rests being
  measured. Nan keeps the bench.
- **Scene 2:** drawings arrive in Dunstan's hand, for a machine with Wilf's arms ("I woke with
  them"). Marit counts the foragers and the pit: for the first time the pit is exactly right. No
  spare notch.
- **Clue:** C-06 (the same cloud twice). **Closing beat:** she recounts three times and finds she
  misses the spare. Dunstan announces that the Spirit has turned its attention to machines, and
  for once he does not add a reason.
- **Satire target:** a surplus used as grounds for a plan. Notch: efficiency begins to describe
  people as parts.
- **Opening image:** a granary with a rite about the door. **Closing beat:** the exact count.
- **Borrowable:** "The count is exact. The Spirit has turned its attention to machines."
