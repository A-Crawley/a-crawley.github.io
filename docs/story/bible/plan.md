## Epoch 1: The Long Afternoon

Ten chapters: story 1 (4), story 2 (3), story 3 (3). The four-movement arc, as a diagnostic.

| Movement        | Share | Chapters            | Job                                                                                      |
| --------------- | ----- | ------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ki (set-up)     | 20%   | 1.1.1, 1.1.2        | The village, its forms, the first wrong detail. Funny, specific.                         |
| Sho (develop)   | 30%   | 1.1.3, 1.1.4, 1.2.1 | Growth and optimisation; drift in small choices. Draught under door.                     |
| Ten (turn)      | 30%   | 1.2.2, 1.2.3, 1.3.1 | Cost to people, contradictions, the reframing (sim confirmed in 1.3.1).                  |
| Ketsu (resolve) | 20%   | 1.3.2, 1.3.3        | Consequences through the new frame, the Apocalypse, a last image that answers the first. |

- Story 1: Marit learns the ledger is the lever (1.1.3), and the count closes exactly (1.1.4).
- Story 2: the supervisor is kept on, and the notices change hand (rough).
- Story 3: Pip pulls the lever (rough).

## Story 1: The Counter (4 chapters)

Narrator: Marit Dunnock. Days 1 to 33. Ends just before machines replace jobs.

### Chapter 1: First Count (written, draft)

- **Movement and promise:** Ki. Opens the promises: where the food comes from; what the lever is;
  whether Marit's count will ever close.
- **Scene 1:** goal, record the first food. Obstacle, there is none and nobody brings any.
  Setback, an empty sack.
- **Scene 2:** goal, close Form 1. Obstacle, a warm loaf with no carrier. Setback, a blank Source
  is "reviewed"; she writes Wilf's name, and he is now the person who must do it again.
- **Clues:** C-01, C-02.
- **Satire target:** the Form with a mandatory Source field. Notch: forms ask for a name, not a fact.
- **Opening image:** an empty pit counted twice. **Closing beat:** she decides to follow Wilf.
- **Borrowable:** Form 1 line, the lever, "Please keep the numbers honest, or at least consistent."

### Chapter 2: The Spare Notch (planned)

- **Movement and promise:** Ki. Day 6. Advances the count promise: it does not close.
- **Goal:** reconcile the first week's intake to the pit. **Obstacle:** the pit is one over her
  notches, every day. **Setback:** Form 2 asks for "variance"; Leadership files hers under growth
  and congratulates her. She does not correct it.
- **Sequel:** rations by output. Wilf eats a full share, Nan a half. The books only balance if
  Marit does not look at where Wilf's bread goes (he gives it to Nan). She does not look.
- **Clues:** C-03 (notices numbered "Day 6" in a village with no calendar), C-04 (Pip says there is
  a number up there and it just went up; Nan says it is cloud).
- **Satire target:** variance as growth; ration by output. Notch: praise as a way to close an
  inquiry.
- **Opening image:** Marit counts the pit with Wilf's finds and gets one more. **Closing beat:** she
  tucks the stick under her arm and says nothing.
- **Borrowable:** "Leadership calls the difference growth." "Rations by output."

### Chapter 3: Onboarding (planned)

- **Movement and promise:** Sho. Day 19. Advances: what Leadership does with her numbers.
- **Scene 1:** four newcomers stand at the crossing with names pinned to them. "Where were you
  before?" "Onboarding." Eleven villagers.
- **Scene 2:** Form 4 ranks associates by output per week, from Marit's ledger. Hester's figure
  sits on a rounding line: 11.5. Whichever way she rounds, someone moves.
- **Setback:** she rounds Hester up. Wilf is now last. **Small turn:** the ledger is the lever.
- **Clue:** C-05. **Drift:** Marit's first rounding with a victim.
- **Satire target:** ranking from a clerk's rounding. Notch: the metric is the person.
- **Opening image:** four strangers wearing their own name tags. **Closing beat:** Notice: "The
  Records Associate is a key dependency." She reads it as praise.
- **Borrowable:** "Onboarding." Name-tag arrivals. Form 4.

### Chapter 4: Exact (planned)

- **Movement and promise:** Sho. Day 33. Pays off the count promise unexpectedly.
- **Scene 1:** granary built, ten foragers, a surplus and a bench. Notice about rests being measured.
  Nan keeps the bench.
- **Scene 2:** drawings arrive "from Research" for a machine with Wilf's arms. Marit counts the
  foragers and the pit: for the first time the pit is exactly right. No spare notch.
- **Clue:** C-06 (the same cloud twice). **Closing beat:** she recounts three times and finds she
  misses the spare. The board says Leadership has turned its attention to machines.
- **Satire target:** a surplus used as grounds for a plan. Notch: efficiency begins to describe
  people as parts.
- **Opening image:** a granary with a form about the door. **Closing beat:** the exact count.
- **Borrowable:** "The count is exact. Leadership has turned its attention to machines."
