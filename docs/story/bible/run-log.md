  newcomers make eleven (this fixes a count that did not add up before); renamed titles (Keeper of
  the Tally, Gatherer, Warden of Timber, Raiser of Walls).

  early is "reading above one's grade", a small impropriety. The Latch opens when the Yield is
  sufficient, and then the Speaker will read the Margin to everyone, in order. Dunstan himself
  never looks up in company and looks up often alone.
- **What the Source business became:** Source is Bearer. The Spirit gives only through hands, so
  any Offering with no named Bearer is Orphaned and Spoken Over at a Hearing (a review). The clue
  is unchanged: a loaf with no carrier, a doctrine insisting there was one, and a clerk who supplies
  a name so the book closes.
- **Open threads:** why Dunstan wakes holding things; when the Hearing is (it "will be
  announced"); what Dunstan hears; the Quiet column.
