## Spread check

- Planted clues per chapter: 1.1.1 has C-01 and C-02; 1.1.2 has C-03 and C-04; 1.1.3 has C-05;
  1.1.4 has C-06. That is four chapters of clues, at most two each, and none in the two chapters
  before the 1.3.1 reveal.
- Dunstan's hand going to his ear, and the ruled bark he wakes holding, are ambient detail inside
  C-01 and are not counted as separate clues. They are never remarked on by the narrator. Their true
  reading (the Word arrives from outside, and he cannot tell where) is paid in 1.3.1 and 1.3.3.
- C-07 (the spare notch) is the plot of 1.1.2 and recurs, so it is counted as a running gag and not
  as a hidden clue. Its true reading is paid in 1.3.3.
