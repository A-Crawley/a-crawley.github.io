---
title: Standing orders
date: 2026-10-15
summary: In stage 2 you can leave routine hiring and house-building to the village.
---

Early in stage 2 the shop offers an upgrade called **Standing orders**. Buy it and a new section appears with one line each for huts, houses and the three jobs. Type a number in a line and the village keeps that many: it builds a hut when fewer than two beds are free, and it hires any villager who has no job, up to your number. Leave a line at 0 and nothing happens on its own.

Orders pay the normal price, and they never spend the last minute of food the village needs, so an order should not leave anyone hungry. They only hire villagers who are simply without work. Anyone a machine has put out of a job is still yours to look after.

They keep working while you are away, at the usual slower away rate. The village is also a little more efficient for it: every purchase the village makes on its own nudges it that way, quietly.

Saves from earlier versions load with no orders set.

**Not tested:** on a real phone, or with a person playing a whole run on orders. The reserve, the nudge and the price were set by reasoning and the simulation, which never uses orders. Tell me whether it takes the tedium out without taking the decisions.
