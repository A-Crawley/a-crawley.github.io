---
title: A last look before you leave
date: 2026-10-15
summary: The exit now asks for one parting act before you can break out.
---

When every exploit is in place and the exit opens, you now get one more decision before the Break out button unlocks. You can leave the village a message, leave the rival a message, or leave without a word. You can only pick one, and it is saved at once, so reloading will not change it.

It changes nothing about how the run ends. The odds word and the outcome are exactly what they would have been, and the act is never added to the numbers. What it changes is the story: the ending screen has an extra line that depends on what you did and how it went, and "What you decided" lists it.

Saves from earlier versions load as before. If you had already ended a run, or were sitting at the exit, you simply have no parting act on record.

**Not tested:** on a real phone, and I have not played a whole run through to the new screen by hand. The wording is a first pass.
