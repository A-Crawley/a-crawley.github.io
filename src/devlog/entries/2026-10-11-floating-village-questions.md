---
title: Village questions no longer shove the page
date: 2026-10-11
summary: When the village asks you something, the question floats over the page instead of pushing it down.
---

A village question used to appear at the top of the page and push everything down by about a third of a screen, then yank it back when you answered. Buttons you were about to press ended up somewhere else.

Now the question floats. On a phone it sits just above the Gather button and the tabs. On a bigger screen it sits in the bottom corner. The rest of the page stays where it was, and you can still gather food while a question is waiting.

**Not tested:** on a real phone. A tall question may cover part of the shop on a small screen until you answer it.
