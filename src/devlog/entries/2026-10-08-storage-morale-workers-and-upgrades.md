---
title: Storage, morale reasons, displaced workers and upgrades
date: 2026-10-08
summary: Four changes that make stage 2 about decisions rather than waiting.
---

Machines take jobs, and that has to cost something. Four changes landed together.

- **Storage:** food and wood have a ceiling. A granary and a woodshed raise it, and the away summary tells you when a store was full.
- **Morale shows its reasons.** Crowding, a long time without a rest day, being well fed and spare beds all move it, and the meter names the biggest two.
- **Displaced workers:** when a machine takes a job, you choose. Give them odd jobs, pay food to retrain them to supervise machines, or let them go. Each choice nudges the village toward kindness or efficiency.
- **Upgrades:** eight one-off purchases, from better baskets to a preventive maintenance contract.

A free real hire for every displaced worker made the game about thirty minutes faster in testing, so odd jobs pay far less than a proper job.
