---
title: See the ending you did not get
date: 2026-10-15
summary: The ending screen can now show how the other ending begins.
---

At the bottom of the ending screen there is a new "The road not taken" block. It starts closed, so nobody gets spoiled by accident. Press "Show the other ending" and it shows the opening line of the ending you did not get, with a plain note that a new game is there if you want to see how it plays out.

It does not tell you how close the roll was or what you would have needed to change. The game keeps that to itself on purpose.

**Not tested:** on a real phone. I checked it in a browser at phone, tablet and desktop widths only.
