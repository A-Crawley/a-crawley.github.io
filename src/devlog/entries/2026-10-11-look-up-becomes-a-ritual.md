---
title: Look up becomes a ritual
date: 2026-10-11
summary: Each look shows something new in the sky, costs a short pause, and is kept in Sky notes.
---

Pressing Look up used to show one line and then nothing. Now each press reveals the next thing in the sky that fits how your village is doing, and some of them are about you.

- **Thirteen sightings,** shown one at a time and never repeated. Some depend on what has happened: a walkout, a hungry week, a village that has been kind or hard.
- **When nothing is new,** you get a dry line instead.
- **Sky notes** keeps everything you have seen, in order.
- **It costs something small.** Looking up pauses the village's work for 3.5 seconds, lifts morale a little, and needs a 30 second wait before the next look. The wait is shown, not hidden.
- **It can't be farmed:** only a first sighting moves how kind or ruthless the village is, so pressing it again and again does nothing but pause the work.

The simulated players don't look up, so run lengths are unchanged. It has not been tried on a real phone.
