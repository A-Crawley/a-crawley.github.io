---
title: The shop holds still when something new appears
date: 2026-10-11
summary: A new row or section in the shop no longer shoves the buttons you are looking at.
---

When the shop revealed a new row, for example a new storage building, everything below it slid down. If you were about to tap a button there, it moved first.

Now the page adjusts by the same amount when a row, section or upgrade appears, so the button you were looking at stays under your thumb. The new item simply appears above or below it.

The empty gap above the tab bar at the bottom of the phone screen is also gone, since Gather food moved into the Build tab.

**Not tested:** on a real phone. I tested the adjusting in an automated test but could not reliably watch a real reveal happen in a browser.
