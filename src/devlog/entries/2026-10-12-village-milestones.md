---
title: The village notices when it gets big
date: 2026-10-12
summary: Reaching round numbers of workers and machines now earns a log line and a brief glow on its shop row.
---

When you reach 25, 50 or 100 of a worker, or 5, 10 or 25 of a machine, the village log now says so, in the voice the village uses for most things. The shop row for that item glows briefly. If you buy a lot at once and pass several round numbers, you get a line for each.

It changes nothing about how the game plays. No bonus, no cheaper prices, nothing faster.

If you were away, only the three biggest new lines are shown when you come back, so the log does not fill up. Saves from before this update are brought across quietly: numbers you had already passed do not get a line.

**Not tested:** on a real phone, and I have not watched all eighteen lines appear in a real run. The wording is a first pass. Under reduced motion the glow is a still highlight instead.
