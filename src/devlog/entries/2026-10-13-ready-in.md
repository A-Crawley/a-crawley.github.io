---
title: Locked items say when they will be ready
date: 2026-10-13
summary: A shop item you cannot afford yet now shows roughly how long until you can.
---

Under the buy button of anything you cannot afford yet there is now a short note, for example "ready in 2m 10s". It counts down, one second at a time, and changes when you switch between ×1, ×10, ×100 and Max, since a bigger purchase takes longer.

Two other notes can appear. "Needs more storage" means the price is more than your store can hold, so waiting will never be enough. "Not at this rate" means your stock is not growing right now, for example because the village is eating as much as it makes.

The time is worked out from what the village makes by itself. It does not count your Gather clicks, rest days or your next purchases, so early on it will often be longer than it turns out to be. Nothing about the economy changed.

**Not tested:** on a real phone. I measured the shop rows in a browser at phone, tablet and desktop widths and none moved, but I have not played a whole run with the notes showing, and the wording is a first pass.
