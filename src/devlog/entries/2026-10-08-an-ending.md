---
title: An ending, achievements and a balance pass
date: 2026-10-08
summary: The final choice and two endings, achievements, a first balance pass and an accessibility pass.
---

The game now has an end. After the last exploit there is one choice, with odds of getting through that depend on how you treated the village along the way. There are two endings, and a screen that shows what your run says about you.

- **Achievements** for the things you did, and a few for the things you could have done.
- **Balance:** rest days were shortened and offline time was set to a quarter after a simulated run came out much too slow for the kind player.
- **Accessibility and phones:** tap targets are at least 44 pixels, the log is a real list, and a stray horizontal scroll on narrow screens is gone. This was checked with an automated tool, not yet with a screen reader.
