title: The first eight villagers have names
date: 2026-10-15
summary: Priya, Marcus and six others now have names, and the village notices when they leave.
---

The first eight villagers to arrive now have names and a job title: Priya in Food Acquisition, Marcus in Timber Operations, Dee, Okafor, Wen, Tomasz, Ines and Abel. Everyone after them is still just a number.

If the village shrinks, whether from hunger, a walkout or you letting someone go, the earliest named villager still here is the one who goes, and the village log says so in its usual dry way. They do not come back. The performance review lists who has left so far, and the ending screen says who stayed.

It is all words. Nothing about food, wood, morale or the ending changes because of the names.

Saves from before this load fine. Everyone who would be around counts as having arrived and nobody is marked as gone.

**Not tested:** on a real phone, and I have not played a run where the village shrinks all the way down to see the ending line for "nobody stayed". Village events do not mention the cast yet. The names and lines are a first pass.
