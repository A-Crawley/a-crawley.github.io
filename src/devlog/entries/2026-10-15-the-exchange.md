---
title: The Exchange
date: 2026-10-15
summary: A new place to swap food for wood and back, for a fee.
---

When one of your stores is nearly full and wood is in play, the village opens an **Exchange**. You can sell a lot of wood for food, or food for wood. It keeps 30% as a handling fee, and every trade makes the next one worse. If you stop trading, the rate recovers over a few minutes.

It is there to get you past a bottleneck, such as a full woodshed while the food store is empty, not to make more. It never trades infrastructure, so it cannot shorten the climb to the breakout. It will also not sell the last quarter of your food store, so nobody goes hungry to pay for it.

Saves from earlier versions load with a fresh rate.

**Not tested:** on a real phone, or with a person who trades a lot. The numbers were reasoned and checked in tests, not tuned against a trading player. If it feels too cheap or too stingy, tell me.
