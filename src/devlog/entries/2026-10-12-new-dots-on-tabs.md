---
title: Dots on the tabs for new things
date: 2026-10-12
summary: On a phone, a small dot now tells you when another tab has something new.
---

On a phone only one tab shows at a time, so a new log line, a new Sky note or a new achievement could go by unseen. Now **Log and more** gets a small dot when something new arrives there. The **Village** tab gets one when the village starts to need you, for example when it goes hungry or villagers are left with nothing to do.

The dot goes away when you open that tab. It does not nag: if the village stays hungry, it will not come back until the trouble has stopped and started again. Dots are not saved, so a fresh load starts with none. They do not move or resize the tabs, and tablets and desktops are unchanged.

**Not tested:** on a real phone, or with a screen reader. The tabs read out as, for example, "Log and more, new".
