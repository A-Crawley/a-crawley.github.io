---
title: Buy buttons stay the same size
date: 2026-10-11
summary: Changing Buy amount or watching a price grow no longer re-wraps the shop text.
---

The buy buttons grew wider when a price got longer, for example when you switched from Buy ×1 to ×10 or a price reached 100. That squeezed the text beside them, so descriptions re-wrapped and every row below jumped.

Now every buy button is the same width, so nothing moves when the number on it changes.

**Not tested:** on a real phone, and with the very longest prices in infrastructure, which may still wrap to a second line.
