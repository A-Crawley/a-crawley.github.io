---
title: Gather food moves into the Build tab
date: 2026-10-11
summary: On a phone, the bottom bar is now only the tabs, and it never changes size.
---

On a phone, the big Gather food button used to sit above the tabs in the bottom bar. When it went away later in the game, the bar shrank and tapping the tabs felt off.

Now the bottom bar is just the three tabs, **Build**, **Village** and **Log and more**, and it is always the same size. Gather food sits at the top of the Build tab instead.

**Changed:** Gather food is no longer on screen while you are on the Village or Log tabs. **Not tested:** on a real phone.
