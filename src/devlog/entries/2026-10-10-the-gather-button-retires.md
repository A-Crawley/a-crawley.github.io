---
title: The Gather button retires, and this dev log
date: 2026-10-10
summary: The big button leaves when clicking stops mattering, and the game now has a public dev log.
---

Clicking Gather food is worth about one food. By the middle of the game the village makes hundreds, so the button was taking the best spot on the screen for almost nothing.

Once clicking is under one percent of your food income, the big button retires and a small one moves into the village panels. It comes back if the village runs out of food or goes hungry, because clicking is the way out of that. In simulated runs this happens somewhere between minute 12 and minute 20.
