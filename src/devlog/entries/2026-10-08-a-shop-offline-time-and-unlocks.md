---
title: A shop, offline time and things that unfold
date: 2026-10-08
summary: Bulk buying, morale and policies, the while-you-were-away screen, number formats and unlocks.
---

The game gained a shop with buying in tens and hundreds, a morale meter, and two policies that trade your villagers' wellbeing for output.

Closing the tab no longer stops the village. When you come back, a summary shows what it made while you were gone. Away time counts for a quarter of normal speed, so leaving it overnight does not skip the game.

- **Number formats:** short, scientific, engineering or plain, chosen in Settings.
- **Unlocks:** the screen starts nearly empty. Panels and buttons appear as you reach them and stay once revealed, even if you spend the thing that revealed them.
