---
title: The village remembers
date: 2026-10-15
summary: Six of your earlier answers to village events now come back later as a line in the log.
---

A few of the choices you make when the village asks you something now get a follow-up. A couple of events later, a line appears in the village log that refers back to what you decided: the stranger you welcomed, the stash you kept, the rest break you refused, the message you ignored, the lean week you cut rations for, the door you locked.

They are only words. Nothing about food, morale or the ending changes because of them, and they only turn up for those choices, so a run can easily see none or a few. If nobody answered an event and the village chose for you, it counts the same.

Saves from earlier versions work as they are. The lines are worked out from the answers you have already given, so an old save may show some straight away.

**Not tested:** on a real phone, or with someone reading the log through a whole run. The wording is a first pass, and I do not yet know whether the callbacks are pointed enough or too pointed. Tell me which.
