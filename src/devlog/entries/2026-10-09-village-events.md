---
title: Village events
date: 2026-10-09
summary: Now and then the village asks you a question, and if you leave it, someone answers for you.
---

Eight events can now interrupt the middle of the game: a lean week, a full larder, a visiting auditor, a hard frost and four more. Each is triggered by the state of the village, not by a dice roll, and each can happen once.

Every event has two choices. The kinder one usually costs food or wood. The other is free and a little harder on morale.

- **Leave it alone** and it decides itself after five minutes, always with the free, less kind choice.
- **Away from the game:** at most one event waits for you, and nothing is decided while you are gone.
- **The log** records what was chosen, and says when nobody answered.

The simulated players ignore events, so the run lengths have not changed. How they feel to play has not been measured.
