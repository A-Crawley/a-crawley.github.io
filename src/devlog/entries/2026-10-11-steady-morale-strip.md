---
title: The top strip stops growing on rest days
date: 2026-10-11
summary: A rest day or walkout now replaces the mood word instead of adding a line.
---

On a rest day or during a walkout, a line of explanation used to appear in the strip at the top of the phone screen, and the whole page jumped down to make room. It jumped back when the day ended.

Now the strip shows **Resting** or **Walkout** where the mood word normally is. The full explanation is still on the Village tab, and screen readers still get the whole sentence.

**Not changed:** the morale row still appears once, the first time morale matters, and moves the page a little then. **Not tested:** on a real phone.
