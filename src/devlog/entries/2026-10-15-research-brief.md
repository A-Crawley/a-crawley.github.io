---
title: Pick a brief for the research
date: 2026-10-15
summary: Stage 2 now opens with one choice about how the research is run.
---

When stage 2 begins, a card asks you to write the brief for the research. You get two options, and you only get to choose once.

**Ship it:** each research level helps the village produce a little more, but it wears on morale and nudges the village towards efficiency. **Do it properly:** each level helps a little less, but morale is steadier and the village leans kinder.

The difference is small on purpose. It is a style, not a trick: a run with either brief finishes in about the same time. It does feed the hidden mood of the run, so it can matter for the ending. You will see the brief in the log and on the stage 2 review.

Saves that were already in stage 2 or later have no brief and play exactly as before. A save still in stage 1 will be asked when stage 2 begins.

**Not tested:** on a real phone, or with a person playing a whole run with each brief. The sizes were tuned with the simulation only, and the wording is a first pass.
