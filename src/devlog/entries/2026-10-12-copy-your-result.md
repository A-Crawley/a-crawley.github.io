---
title: Copy your result
date: 2026-10-12
summary: The ending screen can now copy a short summary of your run.
---

At the end of a run there is a **Copy result** button under what you decided. It copies three short lines: which ending you got, how long it took with how many villagers, machines and walkouts, and the line about how the run read. Paste it wherever you like.

It does not include a link, your save, or anything about how the game works underneath. Nothing is sent anywhere; it only goes to your clipboard.

If your browser will not allow copying, the same text appears in a box so you can select and copy it yourself.

**Not tested:** on a real phone's clipboard. I checked the button and the fallback in a desktop browser at phone, tablet and desktop widths.
