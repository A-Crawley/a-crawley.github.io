---
title: The village asks more questions
date: 2026-10-11
summary: Six new village questions, four in the early game and two near the end.
---

The village used to stay quiet through the long first stage. Now it has things to say.

**Early on:** a stranger asks to stay, someone finds a stash in the woods, two groups argue about whether sitting down counts as work, and a villager keeps looking up at the number in the sky.

**Near the end:** a note arrives from the other side, and someone starts copying the village's methods.

As before, each question comes once, two answers, and the kinder one costs something. If nobody answers, it gets decided for you after five minutes, and not kindly. None of them change how fast the village grows.

**Not tested:** by playing a full run by hand. The wording is a first pass.
