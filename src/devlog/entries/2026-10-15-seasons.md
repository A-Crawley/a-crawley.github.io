---
title: Seasons
date: 2026-10-15
summary: The village now has seasons, with a forecast so winter is never a surprise.
---

A few minutes in, the village starts to have seasons, each lasting three minutes. The Village panel has a new **Season** row that says which one it is, what it does, and when the next one starts.

- **Spring:** new villagers arrive faster.
- **Summer:** food comes in faster.
- **Autumn:** wood comes in faster.
- **Winter:** villagers eat more, and new ones arrive slower. A full granary helps.

It is meant to be mostly about timing, not about more: over a year the gains and losses nearly cancel. If you see "Winter in 1m 30s", that is your cue to fill the stores.

Seasons keep turning while you are away, at the same slower pace as everything else, and being away never costs you food. Saves from earlier versions need nothing: the season comes from how long the village has been going.

**Not tested:** on a real phone, or by a person trying to plan around a winter. The effect sizes were chosen so the simulation still finishes in the same time, and may feel too small. Tell me.
