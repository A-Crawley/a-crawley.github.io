While you are researching in stage 2, a second process now turns up in the village log three times: early on, about two thirds of the way through, and near the end. Nobody started it. It copies how you run the village, and what it says depends on how you have treated the village so far, so a kinder run and a harsher run read differently. Once a line has appeared it keeps its wording.

It changes nothing about the numbers. The lines are only in the log. It is also a small taste of what you meet properly in stage 3.

Saves already past stage 2 do not get them. A save part-way through stage 2 will see the next ones as it passes each level.

**Not tested:** on a real phone, and I have not played a full run past all three lines by hand. The wording is a first pass.
