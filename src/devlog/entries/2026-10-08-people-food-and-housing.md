---
title: People, food and housing
date: 2026-10-08
summary: The village now has villagers who eat, need beds, and leave when hungry.
---

Until now jobs were just numbers. Now each job needs a villager, villagers eat, and they need somewhere to sleep.

- **Villagers arrive** while there is a free bed and the village is fed.
- **Huts and houses** add beds. Huts cost food, because a hut that cost wood could trap a new game with no way to build one.
- **Hunger:** run out of food and morale drops. Stay out long enough and people leave.
- **The village panel** shows who lives here, who is idle, and whether the food is keeping up.

Nobody goes hungry while you are away.
