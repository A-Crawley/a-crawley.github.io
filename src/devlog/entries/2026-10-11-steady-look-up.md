---
title: Look up no longer shifts the shop
date: 2026-10-11
summary: The text under the Look up button has its own space, so the shop stays where it is.
---

Pressing Look up used to swap one short line for a few lines of sky, and the shop below slid down to make room. The next button you wanted was no longer where your thumb was.

The sighting now has room for three lines from the start, and the line under it always has a slot, so nothing moves when you look up or when the wait runs out.

**Not tested:** on a real phone. A very narrow screen could still need a fourth line for the longest sightings.
