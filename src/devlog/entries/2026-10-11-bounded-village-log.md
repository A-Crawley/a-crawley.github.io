---
title: The village log stops pushing the settings down
date: 2026-10-11
summary: On a phone, the village log now scrolls inside its own box once it gets long.
---

On the Log and more tab, every new line in the village log used to push Achievements, Settings and Save and reset further down the page. After a long session, the Reset button could end up a long way from where you last saw it.

The log now stops growing at a fixed height and scrolls inside its own box, newest line at the top. The things below it stay put once the log is full.

**Not changed:** the log is still on the same tab as Save and reset. **Not tested:** on a real phone. The first few lines still make the page a little longer, until the box fills.
