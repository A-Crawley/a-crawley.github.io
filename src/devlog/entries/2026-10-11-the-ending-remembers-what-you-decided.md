---
title: The ending remembers what you decided
date: 2026-10-11
summary: The ending screen now lists your village event choices and hints at what you missed.
---

After the ending, there is now a short **What you decided** section under your stats.

- **Every village event you met,** with the choice you made. If nobody answered one, it says the village decided for you.
- **What you did not see:** a count of the events and achievements this run missed.
- **A nudge about the other ending,** kept vague on purpose. It never shows numbers, chances or how kind or ruthless the village was scored.

Nothing about the game itself changed, and it adds nothing to your save. Starting a new game still asks first. It has not been tried on a real phone.
