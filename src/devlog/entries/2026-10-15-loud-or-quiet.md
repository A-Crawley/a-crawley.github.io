---
title: Loud or quiet exploits
date: 2026-10-15
summary: Stage 3 has a new choice: buy exploits quietly, or cheaply and loudly.
---

In stage 3 there is a new switch in the shop called "Approach", with one option: **Loud exploits**. Off is the quiet way and costs the normal price. On makes each exploit 15% cheaper, but the rival notices sooner, so its messages arrive earlier, and the odds of getting through at the end slip a little. It only changes exploits you buy from then on, so you can flip it back whenever you like.

The slip is small on purpose, about one step on the odds word at most if you go loud for every single exploit. The odds are still only ever shown in words.

Saves from earlier versions load on the quiet route with nothing bought loudly. If you were already at the exit, nothing changes.

**Not tested:** on a real phone, or with a person playing stage 3 both ways. The sizes were tuned with the simulation only, and it is possible the loud route is too small a gain or too tempting. Tell me how it feels.
