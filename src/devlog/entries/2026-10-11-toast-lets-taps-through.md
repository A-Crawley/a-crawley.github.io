---
title: Achievement pop-ups no longer block taps
date: 2026-10-11
summary: The pop-up for a new achievement ignores taps, so you can press what is under it.
---

When an achievement popped up on a phone, it covered a strip of the shop for a few seconds, and a button under it could not be pressed.

The pop-up now lets taps pass straight through, and it sits just above the tab bar instead of floating higher up the screen.

**Not tested:** on a real phone.
