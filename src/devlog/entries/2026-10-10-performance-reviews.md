---
title: Performance reviews at each new stage
date: 2026-10-10
summary: Entering stage 2 and stage 3 now brings a short, dry performance review, and the sim starts to crack.
---

Moving into a new stage used to be one line in the village log. Now it gets a moment: a **Performance review** that sums up the stage you just finished, written the way a manager who has never visited the village would write it.

- **What it uses:** how long the stage took, how many villagers and jobs you have, walkouts and rest days. All real figures from your game.
- **Your style:** one line about how you have been running things, in words only. No numbers.
- **Once only:** dismiss it with one button (or Escape) and it does not come back. The village keeps working behind it.
- **If you were away:** the welcome-back screen comes first, then the review.
- **Stage 3:** the title starts to flicker. If your device asks for reduced motion, it stays still with a fixed faint shadow instead.

Saves from before this update are not shown reviews for stages they already passed. Not yet checked by a person on a real phone or with a screen reader.
