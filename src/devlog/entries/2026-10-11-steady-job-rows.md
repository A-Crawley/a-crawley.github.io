---
title: Job buttons stay put
date: 2026-10-11
summary: The Hire buttons no longer jump when nobody is free to take a job.
---

Hiring used to make the whole list shift. When the last free villager took a job, a long note appeared under each job and everything below it moved, so the next tap often missed.

Each job now keeps a fixed space for that note, and the note is shorter: **Nobody is free. Build beds for more.**

**Not tested:** on a real phone. I checked it with a phone-sized browser and the automated tests only.
