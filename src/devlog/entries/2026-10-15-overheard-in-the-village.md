---
title: Overheard in the village
date: 2026-10-15
summary: Every few minutes the village log now picks up a dry line of overheard chatter.
---

During a long wait the village log now picks up a short, dry line every two to four minutes of play: someone circling back, someone without the bandwidth, a quiet moment filed as a ticket. A few only come up when they fit, such as when the village is hungry, someone has nothing to do, the stores are full, or the machines are running. Stages 2 and 3 have a few of their own.

They are only words. They never change food, wood, morale or anything else, and nothing rewards watching for them. The log keeps the last twelve, so you can scroll back.

If you leave the game for a long time you come back to at most one new line, not a pile of them. Reloading never changes which line you got.

Saves from before this load fine and start with none.

**Not tested:** on a real phone, and I have not read a whole run's worth of chatter in order. The wording is a first pass and some lines will repeat over a long run.
